Biography

About

Korean baritone Junseok (Jason) Hwang is currently studying at the Academy of Vocal Arts in Philadelphia. In the summer of 2026 he returned to the Aspen Music Festival as a Renée Fleming Artist.

He earned his Bachelor of Music from The Juilliard School in 2023, studying with William Burden. At Juilliard he performed Nick Bottom in Britten's A Midsummer Night's Dream, Il Marito in Menotti's Amelia al Ballo and Spinelloccio in Puccini's Gianni Schicchi.

His summer festival appearances include Papageno in Die Zauberflöte at the Hawaii Performing Arts Festival, as well as Chautauqua, Classic Lyric Arts Italy and Aspen.

In 2023 he was a semi-finalist and Special Contract Prize winner at the Cascais International Voice Competition and a finalist in the Giulio Gari Competition. From 2024 to 2026 he completed his mandatory military service in Korea.
